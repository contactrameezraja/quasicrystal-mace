Crystal lookup

The five structures of Vibrations in Quasicrystals, searchable by name, formula, atom count, file name or mp-id. Each card carries the crystallography as parsed (Table 2), the Objective 1 stability diagnostics per potential (Table 5), provenance, the archived repository file, and the structure-specific findings. Data are embedded and the page works offline; the Verify the numbers button on each card recomputes every derivable quantity in your browser and checks it against the stated value, the same gates the dissertation applies at structure entry (§3.2: atom count, composition, volume within two per cent). Companion to the data page; the repository also holds crystal_lookup_app.py, a local tool for the same checks on arbitrary structures.
